## Most useful corrections

1. **Reed at 16 px:** the center and left leaf converge into a hairline seam and the lower central gap is only about 0.8 px after favicon scaling. The mark becomes a compact flower/wheat tuft rather than three confidently separated leaves. See [native icons](native-icons.png), second group. Give its small-size drawing wider, more deliberate channels so the three-leaf construction stays clear at actual size. The professional comparison is the uninterrupted, instantly readable orange silhouette in [Headspace's official header](https://www.headspace.com/), captured in [reference](reference-headspace-header.png); simple marks need equally intentional negative space.

2. **Tideline's large silhouette:** the horizontal heavy bar, short rectangular bottom opening, and rounded enclosure read as a backpack or small appliance before a landscape. See the right column of [desktop](desktop.png). This is a subjective association, but it persists across the small samples. Bring the sun/horizon relationship forward and make the lower area feel like open water; the visible goal is a landscape/window silhouette rather than stacked compartments.

Only two issues warrant changes. Current is a strong lead direction. The page is clear and useful for comparing alternatives; no ornamental additions are needed.
